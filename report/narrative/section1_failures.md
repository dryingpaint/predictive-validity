The registry rarely says why a program stopped. In Phase 1, {{section1.failure_modes.family_share.1.unexplained|pct0}} of stopped programs left neither a stated reason nor a result, which is expected: Phase 1 trials seldom post results and companies seldom explain pausing early assets. In Phase 2 and Phase 3 the share without a reason falls to {{section1.failure_modes.family_share.2.unexplained|pct0}} and {{section1.failure_modes.family_share.3.unexplained|pct0}}. Efficacy is the recorded reason for {{section1.failure_modes.biology.2.efficacy|pct0}} of programs that stopped in Phase 2 and {{section1.failure_modes.biology.3.efficacy|pct0}} of those that stopped in Phase 3: the sponsor said so, or the posted or published primary endpoint was missed. Safety accounts for {{section1.failure_modes.biology.2.safety|pct0}} and {{section1.failure_modes.biology.3.safety|pct0}}: among Phase 2 and Phase 3 stops with an efficacy or safety reason, safety is {{section1.failure_modes.biology.2.safety_of_efficacy_or_safety|pct0}} and {{section1.failure_modes.biology.3.safety_of_efficacy_or_safety|pct0}}, below the 24% Harrison (2016) found among 2013–2015 failures with a disclosed reason. Registry records under-report safety stops relative to company disclosures.

At the level of individual trials, the {{section1.failure_modes.terminations.n|int}} industry trials started in 2015–2025 that stopped early or were withdrawn with a reason give: an explicit business, portfolio or competitive reason {{section1.failure_modes.terminations.business|pct0}}, a bare "sponsor decision" {{section1.failure_modes.terminations.sponsor_decision|pct0}}, efficacy {{section1.failure_modes.terminations.efficacy|pct0}}, enrollment or operations {{section1.failure_modes.terminations.enrollment_operational|pct0}} and safety {{section1.failure_modes.terminations.safety|pct0}}. The draft's 44% "commercial or strategic" pooled the first two; a "sponsor decision" with no reason may or may not hide an efficacy result, and the record cannot tell.

Finally, {{section1.failure_modes.family_share.3.other|pct0}} of Phase 3 programs that stopped short of FDA approval had met their primary endpoint. A reading of a sample shows most of them were approved outside the US (Japan, China, Korea, Russia) or received a complete response letter from the FDA: a positive Phase 3 readout is necessary but not sufficient for US approval.
