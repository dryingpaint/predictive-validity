### How polygenicity was measured and tested

**The unit.** Diseases with a US-developed Phase 2 entry in 2008–2019, outside oncology and infectious disease, on programme build {{polygenicity.build_id}}: {{polygenicity.cohort.diseases|int}} diseases with {{polygenicity.cohort.entries|int}} Phase 2 and 3 entries ({{polygenicity.cohort.phase2|int}} Phase 2), of which {{polygenicity.cohort.with_target|int}} have a resolved primary target. The tertiles, their membership and every definition below were fixed in a dated comment on issue #147 ({{polygenicity.comment_id}}) before any outcome was joined.

**The score.** For each disease, the GWAS Catalog (release 2026-11-19) study of the disease, or failing that of a subtype, with public harmonised summary statistics, at least 90% European ancestry, a case-control design and a genome-wide platform (over 200,000 SNPs), taking the largest by effective sample size (4 / (1/cases + 1/controls)). LDSC (the Python 3 port, 2.0.1) with the European LD scores on the HapMap3 SNPs gave the SNP heritability on the observed scale, converted to the liability scale with the study's case fraction and the disease's population prevalence: the GBD 2021 global prevalence of the matching cause (all ages, both sexes, the GBD 2023 round, mapped by hand as the WHO causes are), or where GBD has no cause, the Pan-UK Biobank European case fraction of the matching ICD-10 code. The Pan-UKB code was taken as the code with most cases among the disease's own ICD-10 cross-references, those of its MONDO descendants, and their three-character parents, in one pool, with ranges such as I10–I16 left unexpanded. That was a defect: 30 of the 64 Pan-UKB prevalences came from a code that was neither the disease's own nor its parent (glaucoma's H40 for hypertensive disorder; hyperlipidaemia's E78 for cardiomyopathy), and the comment fixing the definitions, which called 45 of them matches through the three-character parent, was wrong. The registered membership stands as fixed, and the corrected-K sensitivity below (decision of 2026-11-26) takes a disease's own codes first, with ranges expanded, which changes the code for {{polygenicity.k_corrected.code_changed|int}} of the 64. Independent loci are the lead SNPs after PLINK clumping of the same summary statistics at p < 5 × 10⁻⁸, r² < 0.1 within 1 Mb against the European 1000 Genomes panel cut to HapMap3 SNPs, with the extended MHC counted once. The score is loci per unit of liability-scale heritability; a disease scores only when the heritability estimate has z ≥ 4 and the liability-scale value lies in (0, 1]. Tertile cutpoints over the scored diseases: {{polygenicity.cutpoints.score.lower|x2}} and {{polygenicity.cutpoints.score.upper|x2}}. **The registered tests.** P1: among Phase 2 and 3 entries with a resolved primary target, a stop in the "target not causal" failure mode, top tertile against bottom, from a modified Poisson model adjusted as the target-origin test is (disease area, modality, entry period, novelty and phase), with errors clustered by disease. P2: among Phase 2 entries, advance to Phase 3 or approval, adjusted for area, modality, entry period, novelty and precedent. P3: on the same entries, the interaction of the top tertile with human genetic support for the disease (the G3 evidence line: GWAS, gene burden or Mendelian evidence) on advance, from one model holding both and their product, with the genetics ratio within each tertile reported beside it when the test is estimable; it is estimable only when each of the four cells holds at least 15 target sets and one event. Each p is one-sided in the registered direction (more not-causal stops, fewer advances, a smaller genetics benefit) and Holm's correction runs across the three. The middle tertile is reported, in no test.

**The sensitivities, as amended on 2026-11-24.** GWAS size adds the study's log10 effective sample size (4 / (1/cases + 1/controls)) to the covariates and repeats P1–P3 with Holm within itself. Mendelian form splits all {{polygenicity.cohort.diseases|int}} unit diseases, the no-score group included, by whether a gene carries Orphanet, ClinGen, Genomics England or Gene2Phenotype evidence for the disease, a subtype or a disease at Lin ≥ 0.8 in the full Open Targets association file, with "no Mendelian form" in place of the top tertile, again P1–P3 with Holm within. The negative control is P1's model with the outcome a stop whose traced cause is a sponsor decision or operational, entries with a safety stop left out, reported two-sided outside any family; no association is expected, and one would point to confounding by disease area or sponsor interest. The continuous-score and concentration sensitivities registered on 2026-11-23 were dropped in that decision. The corrected-K sensitivity (decision of 2026-11-26) re-chooses the Pan-UKB code own-first with ranges expanded, recomputes the liability-scale heritability and the score from the same LDSC run, re-cuts the tertiles over the {{polygenicity.k_corrected.scored_diseases|int}} diseases scored under it (cutpoints {{polygenicity.k_corrected.cutpoints.lower|x2}} and {{polygenicity.k_corrected.cutpoints.upper|x2}}; {{polygenicity.k_corrected.changes.changed|int}} diseases change tertile, {{polygenicity.k_corrected.changes.between_tertiles|int}} between tertiles, {{polygenicity.k_corrected.changes.lost_score|int}} out of the scored set and {{polygenicity.k_corrected.changes.gained_score|int}} into it) and repeats P1–P3 with Holm within; the registered tertiles stay the primary. The post hoc of the same decision, added after the readings and outside every family, regresses log10 score on log10 effective sample size over the {{polygenicity.post_hoc.residual_gwas_size.diseases|int}} scored diseases (slope {{polygenicity.post_hoc.residual_gwas_size.slope|x2}}, r² {{polygenicity.post_hoc.residual_gwas_size.r2|x2}}), cuts tertiles on the residual ({{polygenicity.post_hoc.residual_gwas_size.changes.changed|int}} diseases change tertile) and reports P1 and P2 top against bottom with two-sided p and no Holm. Matching on sample size was not done: with {{polygenicity.cohort.scored_diseases|int}} diseases it would discard too much. An independent check of every prevalence code on the corrected profile (2026-11-27) graded the 114 scored diseases' codes against the Pan-UKB descriptions and the GBD cause names: 55 correct, 38 defensible (a parent or the main subtype), 12 doubtful and 9 wrong, with 31 prevalences more than fivefold off for a European lifetime-diagnosis GWAS. Two causes are structural: Pan-UKB's ICD-10 list of 921 codes has no I10, so hypertensive disorder takes hypertensive renal disease (I12, prevalence 0.004 against 0.22 by Pan-UKB's own hypertension phenotype); and a subtype's three-character code is taken before the parent of another subtype's four-character code (cardiac rhythm disease takes cardiac arrest over atrial fibrillation). GBD's global, all-age, both-sex point prevalence also sits 5× to 46× under UK lifetime rates for 16 of the 50 diseases that use it. The corrected-K sensitivity is therefore a partial correction, reported as such. A third rule (v3), announced on issue #147 on 2026-11-27 and run as a further sensitivity, with the registered tertiles still the primary: the disease's own three-character ICD-10 codes and the Pan-UKB phecodes reached through its ICD-9 cross-references (the exact phecode, else the integer part) form one pool, then the three-character parents of its own codes; a pick with at least 1,000 European cases is taken before GBD, otherwise GBD when the disease maps to a cause, otherwise the pick when it has at least 100 cases; then the subtypes' codes, their parents and their phecodes as one pool; a chapter-level term (an own cross-reference spanning ten or more codes, or more than thirty subtype codes) gets no prevalence. It scores {{polygenicity.k_v3.scored_diseases|int}} diseases, {{polygenicity.k_v3.category_scored|int}} of the registered scored set dropping out as chapter terms; it changes the prevalence source or code for {{polygenicity.k_v3.code_changed|int}} of the {{polygenicity.cohort.scored_diseases|int}}; tertiles re-cut at {{polygenicity.k_v3.cutpoints.lower|x2}} and {{polygenicity.k_v3.cutpoints.upper|x2}} move {{polygenicity.k_v3.changes.changed|int}} diseases ({{polygenicity.k_v3.changes.between_tertiles|int}} between tertiles, {{polygenicity.k_v3.changes.lost_score|int}} out of the scored set, {{polygenicity.k_v3.changes.gained_score|int}} into it). The independent check, repeated on v3's codes (2026-11-28), graded 23 of the 114 wrong and 13 doubtful (the corrected rule: 9 and 12): the ICD-9 numbers and the phecode numbers coincide for some diseases and not others (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease reaches asthma, lupus reaches osteomyelitis), the most-cases rule prefers a parent bucket to an exact code in the same pool, and the category rule drops glaucoma, hypertensive disorder and cardiomyopathy. v3 fixed 11 of the 21 diseases flagged under the corrected rule and most of the GBD magnitude problems, and introduced more than it fixed; it is reported here and not recommended. Each rule's prevalence, code and score for every disease is stored beside the registered one, so any of them can be taken up without rebuilding.
