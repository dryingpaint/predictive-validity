**Human genetics predicts at every step.** Programs with genetic evidence for their disease reached approval {{section2.rs.genetic_any.rs|x2}} times as often ({{section2.rs.genetic_any.rs_lo|x2}}–{{section2.rs.genetic_any.rs_hi|x2}}), rare-variant and Mendelian evidence ({{section2.rs.genetic_rare.rs|x2}}×) more than GWAS ({{section2.rs.genetic_gwas.rs|x2}}×), as Minikel et al. (2024) found. At Phase 2 it is the strongest preclinical predictor ({{section3.types.germline.rr|x2}}×; Holm-corrected p {{section3.types.germline.holm_p|p}}), and rare-variant evidence keeps an independent effect with every other line held fixed ({{section2.joint_rr.genetic_rare_2.rr|x2}}×). The effect sits where efficacy is tested: genetics adds little at Phase 1 and about 1.5-fold at Phase 2 and Phase 3 (appendix).

**Other evidence predicts less, and partly through genetics.** Somatic drivers ({{section2.rs.somatic.rs|x2}}× to approval; {{section3.types.somatic.rr|x2}}× at Phase 2 in oncology) keep an effect with the other lines held fixed ({{section2.joint_rr.somatic_2.rr|x2}}×). Animal models pass the Phase 2 test ({{section3.types.animal.rr|x2}}×) but add little once genetics is known ({{section2.joint_rr.animal_model_2.rr|x2}}×): they mostly sit on genetically supported targets. Reactome pathways go with approval ({{section2.rs.affected_pathway.rs|x2}}×, {{section2.rs.affected_pathway.n_with|int}} programs) but not reliably at Phase 2; expression in the disease and target liabilities carry nothing. An approved drug on the target for a related disease, the benchmark, predicts more strongly than any preclinical kind ({{section3.types.benchmark.rr|x2}}× at Phase 2).

**Known only after entry.** CRISPR screens and DepMap's essential-gene list were first published in 2019, too late for any program in the window. With hindsight, screen hits go with success ({{section2.rs_untimed.cell_line_screen_untimed.rs|x2}}× to approval), mostly on oncology targets already validated, and essential targets with failure ({{section2.rs_untimed.pan_essential_untimed.rs|x2}}×), much of it from targets found essential after their programs had failed. The genetics result itself is not hindsight: counting evidence known by 2026 instead of before entry weakens it to {{section2.rs_untimed.genetic_any_untimed.rs|x2}}×.
