A drug that lowers its target's function should help when loss of function protects against the disease or gain of function causes it. To test this, an LLM labelled each target–disease pair, without being told any drug, with the direction of its disease-causing and protective variants. Sponsors almost never develop against an established mechanism: of the Phase 2 programs with genetic support, {{section3.mechanism.levels.concordant.programs|int}} acted in the direction the genetics favoured and {{section3.mechanism.levels.discordant.programs|int}} against it. Concordant programs advanced no more often than those whose genetics had no established direction ({{section3.mechanism.levels.concordant.s4|pct0}} against {{section3.mechanism.levels.undirected.s4|pct0}}), and mouse models pointing the drug's way added nothing once genetics was known.
