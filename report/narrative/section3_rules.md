**Three kinds of preclinical evidence predict.** Phase 2 programs whose target had germline genetic evidence for the disease advanced {{section3.types.germline.rr|x2}} times as often as programs without it ({{section3.types.germline.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.germline.hi|x2}}; Holm-corrected p {{section3.types.germline.holm_p|p}}). Somatic drivers in oncology ({{section3.types.somatic.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.somatic.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.somatic.hi|x2}}) and animal models of the disease ({{section3.types.animal.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.animal.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.animal.hi|x2}}) also survive the correction, with smaller effects. Animal models predict largely because they sit on genetically supported targets: holding the other lines fixed, mouse-model evidence adds little (Section 2). Reactome pathways ({{section3.types.pathway.rr|x2}}) point the same way but do not survive the correction, and expression ({{section3.types.expression.rr|x2}}) and target liabilities ({{section3.types.liability.rr|x2}}) carry nothing. The benchmark is stronger than any preclinical kind ({{section3.types.benchmark.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.benchmark.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.benchmark.hi|x2}}).

**Being more specific does not help.** No narrower version beats the rest of its kind: not strong GWAS evidence ({{section3.refinements.gwas_strong.rr|x2}}), Mendelian evidence ({{section3.refinements.mendelian_nonhereditary.rr|x2}}) or genetics pointing the drug's way ({{section3.refinements.mechanism_concordant.rr|x2}}) within germline genetics, nor a close phenotype match ({{section3.refinements.animal_close.rr|x2}}) or a mouse model pointing the drug's way ({{section3.refinements.animal_direction.rr|x2}}) within animal models, nor a driver matching the drug within somatic drivers ({{section3.refinements.somatic_matching.rr|x2}}).

**Known only after entry.** CRISPR screens and DepMap's essential-gene list date from 2019, too late for this cohort; with hindsight, a screen hit went with advancing ({{section3.hindsight.cell_screen.rr|x2}}) and an essential target with not advancing ({{section3.hindsight.pan_essential.rr|x2}}).
