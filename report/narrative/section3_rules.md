**Three kinds of preclinical evidence predict.** Phase 2 programs whose target had germline genetic evidence for the disease advanced {{section3.types.germline.rr|x2}} times as often as programs without it ({{section3.types.germline.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.germline.hi|x2}}; Holm-corrected p {{section3.types.germline.holm_p|p}}). Somatic drivers in oncology ({{section3.types.somatic.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.somatic.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.somatic.hi|x2}}) and animal models of the disease ({{section3.types.animal.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.animal.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.animal.hi|x2}}) also survive the correction, with smaller effects. Reactome pathways ({{section3.types.pathway.rr|x2}}) point the same way but do not, and expression ({{section3.types.expression.rr|x2}}) and target liabilities ({{section3.types.liability.rr|x2}}) carry nothing. The benchmark is stronger than any preclinical kind ({{section3.types.benchmark.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.benchmark.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.benchmark.hi|x2}}).

**Being more specific does not help.** No narrower version beats the rest of its own kind. Among programs with germline genetics, strong GWAS evidence ({{section3.refinements.gwas_strong.rr|x2}}), Mendelian evidence in a non-hereditary disease ({{section3.refinements.mendelian_nonhereditary.rr|x2}}) and genetics pointing the same way as the drug ({{section3.refinements.mechanism_concordant.rr|x2}}) advanced about as often as the rest. Among programs with an animal model, a close phenotype match ({{section3.refinements.animal_close.rr|x2}}) or a model pointing the drug's way ({{section3.refinements.animal_direction.rr|x2}}) added nothing, nor did a somatic driver matching the drug among oncology programs with a driver ({{section3.refinements.somatic_matching.rr|x2}}). Protective loss of function plus an inhibitor covered {{section3.refinements.protective_inhibitor.n|int}} programs, too few targets to test.

**Known only after entry.** Cancer cell-line CRISPR screens and DepMap's list of essential genes were first published in 2019, after every program in the window had entered Phase 2, so no sponsor could have used them. Scored on today's data, as hindsight, a screen hit went with advancing ({{section3.hindsight.cell_screen.rr|x2}}, {{section3.hindsight.cell_screen.lo|x2}}–{{section3.hindsight.cell_screen.hi|x2}}; {{section3.hindsight.cell_screen.n|int}} programs) and an essential target with not advancing ({{section3.hindsight.pan_essential.rr|x2}}, {{section3.hindsight.pan_essential.lo|x2}}–{{section3.hindsight.pan_essential.hi|x2}}); both partly reflect what the screens learned from drugs that had already succeeded or failed.
