**Germline genetics is the one kind of preclinical evidence that predicts.** Phase 2 programs whose target had germline genetic evidence for the disease advanced {{section3.types.germline.rr|x2}} times as often as programs without it ({{section3.types.germline.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.germline.hi|x2}}; Holm-corrected p {{section3.types.germline.holm_p|p}}). Animal models ({{section3.types.animal.rr|x2}}), somatic drivers in oncology ({{section3.types.somatic.rr|x2}}) and Reactome pathways ({{section3.types.pathway.rr|x2}}) point the same way, but none survives the correction; expression ({{section3.types.expression.rr|x2}}) and target liabilities ({{section3.types.liability.rr|x2}}) carry nothing. Cell screens could not be tested: Project Score was first released in 2019, and only {{section3.types.cell_screen.n|int}} programs in this cohort had a screen before entry. The benchmark is stronger than any preclinical kind ({{section3.types.benchmark.rr|x2}}, {{section3.types.benchmark.lo|x2}}–{{section3.types.benchmark.hi|x2}}).

**Being more specific does not help.** No narrower version beats the rest of its own kind. Among programs with germline genetics, strong GWAS evidence ({{section3.refinements.gwas_strong.rr|x2}}), Mendelian evidence in a non-hereditary disease ({{section3.refinements.mendelian_nonhereditary.rr|x2}}) and genetics pointing the same way as the drug ({{section3.refinements.mechanism_concordant.rr|x2}}) advanced about as often as the rest. Among programs with an animal model, a close phenotype match ({{section3.refinements.animal_close.rr|x2}}) or a model pointing the drug's way ({{section3.refinements.animal_direction.rr|x2}}) added nothing, nor did a somatic driver matching the drug among oncology programs with a driver ({{section3.refinements.somatic_matching.rr|x2}}). Protective loss of function plus an inhibitor covered {{section3.refinements.protective_inhibitor.n|int}} programs, too few targets to test. The rules that looked best in the registered screen did so because they sit inside germline genetics, not because their extra condition adds anything.
