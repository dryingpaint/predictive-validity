## Methods: which diseases the target-based paradigm solves

**Diseases.** Eighteen diseases were shortlisted because several approved drugs are measured on the same endpoint there: twelve on a responder goal, five on events. A disease stayed only if drugs on at least three distinct pathways carry an FDA label indicating them for it, of any approval year. Hidradenitis suppurativa failed this with two (TNF, IL-17). A language model read each label's indications to match it to a disease, and named the signalling pathways of each drug's mechanism: a ligand with its receptors, the cascade the target sits in, the cell population a depleting drug removes, or otherwise the target's family.

**Estimates.** For each approved or Phase 3-positive regimen, a language model with web search took the placebo-adjusted effect on the registered endpoint from the latest network meta-analysis covering it, or else from pooled placebo-controlled Phase 3 trials, in patients new to advanced therapy. Biomarker-selected trials and subgroup estimates were left out. Patients who had failed a named earlier drug gave the crossover estimates. For event diseases it took each drug's hazard ratio against placebo and the hazard ratio of each drug added to the ones before it, with trial length, and for each risk factor the Mendelian randomisation estimate of lifelong lowering. 330 estimates were stored. In a random sample of 15, the 10 with a reachable source matched it; the other 5 were paywalled.

**Labels.** Solved by one drug: one molecule acting through at most three pathways reaches a placebo-adjusted 50% (of patients at goal, or of events prevented). Solved by stacking: the best multi-drug regimen reaches 50%, with stacked hazard ratios multiplied. Labels are also shown at 40% and 60%. For diseases one drug doesn't solve, the retained effect is a drug's placebo-adjusted effect in patients who failed another pathway divided by its effect in new patients; a median of at least 0.75 means different patients, below 0.50 with a randomised win of two pathways over one means several targets, and anything else can't tell. All rules were registered before any estimate was collected.
