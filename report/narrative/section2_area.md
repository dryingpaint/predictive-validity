Human genetic evidence predicts approval in every group of disease areas where it can be measured, but not equally: whether its effect at Phase 2 differs between groups gives p = {{section2.area.tests.genetic_any.p|x2}}. It is strongest in the smaller areas pooled as "other" (ophthalmology, infectious disease, urology and the diseases outside the named areas, mostly kidney, skin and rare hereditary disorders), where programs with it reached approval {{section2.area.rs.other_genetic_any.rs|x2}} times as often ({{section2.area.rs.other_genetic_any.loa_with|pct}} against {{section2.area.rs.other_genetic_any.loa_without|pct}}), and in neuroscience ({{section2.area.rs.neuroscience_genetic_any.rs|x2}}, carried by rare-variant evidence at {{section2.area.rs.neuroscience_genetic_rare.rs|x2}}). It is about threefold in cardiometabolic and endocrine diseases ({{section2.area.rs.cardiometabolic_genetic_any.rs|x2}}), twofold in oncology ({{section2.area.rs.oncology_genetic_any.rs|x2}}), and weakest in immunology and inflammation ({{section2.area.rs.immunology_genetic_any.rs|x2}}, {{section2.area.rs.immunology_genetic_any.rs_lo|x2}}–{{section2.area.rs.immunology_genetic_any.rs_hi|x2}}), where most of the evidence is GWAS. Hematology has too few programs with genetic evidence ({{section2.area.rs.hematology_genetic_any.n_with|int}}) to estimate.

No other line's effect measurably differs between groups. In oncology, a somatic driver gives {{section2.area.rs.oncology_somatic.rs|x2}} ({{section2.area.rs.oncology_somatic.rs_lo|x2}}–{{section2.area.rs.oncology_somatic.rs_hi|x2}}). A potent drug gives {{section2.area.rs.other_assay_potent.rs|x2}} in the "other" group and {{section2.area.rs.neuroscience_assay_potent.rs|x2}} in neuroscience, but the groups do not differ beyond chance (p = {{section2.area.tests.assay_potent.p|x2}}). Leaving out any one group moves the pooled relative success of human genetics between {{section2.area.loo_min.rs|x2}} and {{section2.area.loo_max|x2}}; it is lowest without the "other" group, so no single area carries the pooled result.
