### Why programs stop

For {{stop_causes.inferred.programs|int}} of the {{stop_causes.stops|int}} Phase 2 and 3 stops among 2008–2019 entries, the registry gives no usable reason: none at all, only "sponsor decision", an unspecified "other" reason, a mixed primary endpoint, or an efficacy reason that the reading of efficacy failures below found wrong. An LLM traced each of these through the program's trials and a web search of company releases, filings and news. It found a sourced cause for {{stop_causes.inferred.resolved|pct0}} and a likely cause for {{stop_causes.inferred.likely_resolved|pct0}}. How it works, and how well it does on stops whose reason is known, is in the appendix.

**Some recorded stops were not stops.** Tracing found that {{stop_causes.not_a_stop.not_stopped|int}} Phase 2 and 3 programs the registry shows as stopped had carried on: under another registry, region, name or combination, or through FDA approval for the disease. Another {{stop_causes.not_a_stop.not_a_program|int}} were never development programs: academic studies using an approved drug as a research tool, or drug-interaction and dosing studies. The cause shares in this section leave all of them out. The success rates above rely on ClinicalTrials.gov alone and count them as stops, so those rates are slightly low: counting what tracing found (a later phase as advancing, development still under way as still running, and non-programs not at all) raises the likelihood of approval from {{section1.overall.loa|pct}} to {{section1.traced.loa|pct}} across all programs, and from {{section1.us_site.loa|pct}} to {{section1.traced_us.loa|pct}} for programs developed in the US (appendix).

**Efficacy is the main reason programs stop in Phases 2 and 3.** Counting sourced and likely causes, efficacy or exposure ended {{stop_causes.by_phase.2.shown.efficacy.share|pct0}} of Phase 2 stops and {{stop_causes.by_phase.3.shown.efficacy.share|pct0}} of Phase 3 stops, against {{stop_causes.recorded.efficacy.share|pct0}} of Phase 2 and 3 stops as the registry records them. Sponsor decisions are the next largest cause in Phase 2 ({{stop_causes.by_phase.2.shown.sponsor_decision.share|pct0}}) and the largest in Phase 1 ({{stop_causes.by_phase.1.shown.sponsor_decision.share|pct0}}). In Phase 3, {{stop_causes.by_phase.3.shown.positive_not_advanced.share|pct0}} of stops followed a positive readout. Safety is rare once only stops whose sponsor gives a safety reason count: {{stop_causes.by_phase.2.shown.safety.share|pct0}} in Phase 2 and {{stop_causes.by_phase.3.shown.safety.share|pct0}} in Phase 3.

**What each cause breaks down into.** Shares are of Phase 2 and 3 stops whose cause is sourced.

* **Sponsor decisions** most often put other programs first ({{stop_causes.subcauses.sponsor_decision.portfolio_prioritisation.share|pct0}}) or followed the sponsor running out of money or closing ({{stop_causes.subcauses.sponsor_decision.funding_or_closure.share|pct0}}); a partner leaving ({{stop_causes.subcauses.sponsor_decision.partner_exit.share|pct0}}) and commercial or competitive reasons ({{stop_causes.subcauses.sponsor_decision.commercial_or_competitive.share|pct0}}) come next, and {{stop_causes.subcauses.sponsor_decision.not_stated.share|pct0}} say no more than "business reasons". For {{stop_causes.likely_only.sponsor_decision|pct0}} of all sponsor-decision stops the cause itself is only likely, so they have no subcause.
* **Positive readouts that went no further** mostly did so for the sponsor's own reasons ({{stop_causes.positive_discontinued|pct0}} in all), chiefly putting other programs first ({{stop_causes.subcauses.positive_not_advanced.portfolio_prioritisation.share|pct0}}). In {{stop_causes.subcauses.positive_not_advanced.approved_outside_us.share|pct0}} the drug was approved outside the US, and in {{stop_causes.subcauses.positive_not_advanced.regulator_declined.share|pct0}} a regulator declined it; {{stop_causes.subcauses.positive_not_advanced.not_stated.share|pct0}} give no reason. For {{stop_causes.named.positive_not_advanced.likely|pct0}} the reason is likely rather than stated by a source.
* **Safety stops** were mostly harms seen in patients whose mechanism no source states ({{stop_causes.subcauses.safety.harm_mechanism_not_stated.share|pct0}}). Off-target toxicity ({{stop_causes.subcauses.safety.off_target_toxicity.share|pct0}}), animal toxicology findings ({{stop_causes.subcauses.safety.animal_toxicology.share|pct0}}) and on-target toxicity ({{stop_causes.subcauses.safety.on_target_toxicity.share|pct0}}) are the rest.
* **Operational stops** are mostly slow enrollment ({{stop_causes.subcauses.operational.enrollment.share|pct0}}), then COVID-19 ({{stop_causes.subcauses.operational.covid_19.share|pct0}}) and trials redesigned or replaced ({{stop_causes.subcauses.operational.trial_redesigned.share|pct0}}).
* **Efficacy failures** are broken down in the next section, by whether the drug engaged its target.
* **Stops no source explains**: {{stop_causes.subcauses.unknown.sponsor_went_quiet.share|pct0}} had a sponsor that started no later trial, and in {{stop_causes.subcauses.unknown.drug_dropped.share|pct0}} the drug was dropped while its sponsor carried on.

**Over time.** Counting sourced and likely causes, and leaving out records that were not stops, efficacy or exposure was the cause of {{stop_causes.by_period.2008-2011.shown.efficacy.share|pct0}} of stops among programs entering in 2008–2011, {{stop_causes.by_period.2012-2015.shown.efficacy.share|pct0}} in 2012–2015 and {{stop_causes.by_period.2016-2019.shown.efficacy.share|pct0}} in 2016–2019; sponsor decisions {{stop_causes.by_period.2008-2011.shown.sponsor_decision.share|pct0}}, {{stop_causes.by_period.2012-2015.shown.sponsor_decision.share|pct0}} and {{stop_causes.by_period.2016-2019.shown.sponsor_decision.share|pct0}}, and a positive readout not yet advanced {{stop_causes.by_period.2008-2011.shown.positive_not_advanced.share|pct0}}, {{stop_causes.by_period.2012-2015.shown.positive_not_advanced.share|pct0}} and {{stop_causes.by_period.2016-2019.shown.positive_not_advanced.share|pct0}}. Among entries from 2015 to 2025 ({{stop_causes.recent.stops|int}} stops, many later programs still running), the shares are {{stop_causes.recent.shown.efficacy.share|pct0}} efficacy or exposure, {{stop_causes.recent.shown.sponsor_decision.share|pct0}} sponsor decision and {{stop_causes.recent.shown.positive_not_advanced.share|pct0}} positive readouts.

Safety counts only stops whose sponsor states a safety reason, and the classifier, tested on stops whose reason is known, often read safety stops as efficacy. Safety is therefore under-counted and the efficacy share is an upper estimate.
