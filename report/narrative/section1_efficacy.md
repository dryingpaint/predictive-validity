### Why efficacy failed: the target was not engaged, or it was the wrong target

Efficacy fails in one of two ways: the drug never engaged its target in patients, so the hypothesis went untested, or it engaged and the disease did not respond. For every program entering Phase 1, 2 or 3 in 2008–2019 that stopped for efficacy or exposure (PK, dose or formulation), {{attribution.phase1.programs|int}} in Phase 1 and {{attribution.phase23.programs|int}} in Phase 2 or 3, an LLM read the trials, results, papers and, where those were silent, the web, and judged whether the drug reached and engaged its target in humans at the doses tested. The method and its checks are in the appendix.

The efficacy panels of the figure above break these failures down by phase. Where the record shows whether the mechanism was tested, the drug had engaged its target in {{attribution.phase23.subcauses.engaged_no_benefit.n|int}} of {{attribution.phase23.testable.n|int}} programs and the disease did not respond; **{{attribution.phase23.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}** ({{attribution.phase23.testable.lo|pct0}}–{{attribution.phase23.testable.hi|pct0}}) failed without adequate engagement or exposure, or with another drug on the same target succeeding in the disease. The second kind is inferred rather than measured: {{attribution.phase23.details.other_drug_on_target_worked.n|int}} of the {{attribution.phase23.subcauses.not_engaged.n|int}} not-engaged programs failed where a drug on the same target worked, {{attribution.phase23.other_drug_worked_no_engagement_data|int}} of them with no human engagement data of their own; the rest had exposure or engagement measured and found inadequate. Failures to engage are about as common in Phase 3 ({{attribution.by_phase.3.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}) as in Phase 2 ({{attribution.by_phase.2.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}): no regulation requires engagement to be shown, and good practice places it in Phase 1 or 2a, yet engagement was often still unverified when confirmatory trials started. Outside oncology the share is {{attribution.outside_oncology.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}. Trial design, population or endpoint account for {{attribution.phase23.subcauses.trial_design.share|pct0}} of efficacy failures, and toxicity that offset efficacy for {{attribution.phase23.subcauses.toxicity_offset.n|int}} programs. Another {{attribution.reclassified.programs|int}} programs recorded as efficacy stops were not failed efficacy tests (a met endpoint, a missed non-inferiority margin, a business stop, or off-target toxicity); they are counted under their traced cause above and left out here.

* *Engaged, no benefit.* Venglustat cut glucosylceramide in the cerebrospinal fluid of people with *GBA1* Parkinson's disease by about 73% at the top dose ([Peterschmitt 2022](https://doi.org/10.3233/JPD-212714)), and over a year their motor scores worsened by 7.3 points on drug against 4.7 on placebo ([MOVES-PD](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00205-3)). CSL112, plasma-derived apolipoprotein A1 that raises cholesterol efflux capacity, did not reduce myocardial infarction, stroke or cardiovascular death at 90 days in 18,219 patients after myocardial infarction (hazard ratio 0.93, 0.81–1.05; [AEGIS-II](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2400969)).
* *Not engaged.* PF-06687234, an IL-10 fusion protein added to infliximab for ulcerative colitis, was stopped for futility, and was detected, at low concentration, in only one colon tissue sample: the drug may never have reached the inflamed mucosa at a useful level ([Ther Adv Gastroenterol 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/17562848251410784)).

**Phase 1.** Phase 1 trials mostly test safety, dose and exposure, often in healthy volunteers, so an efficacy or exposure stop there is usually an early patient cohort or a dose-finding readout. The same labels were applied to the {{attribution.phase1.programs|int}} Phase 1 programs entering 2008–2019 that stopped for efficacy or exposure. Where engagement is known ({{attribution.phase1.testable.n|int}} programs), {{attribution.phase1.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}} ({{attribution.phase1.testable.lo|pct0}}–{{attribution.phase1.testable.hi|pct0}}) were not engaged; {{attribution.phase1.subcauses.no_engagement_data.share|pct0}} have no engagement evidence either way.
