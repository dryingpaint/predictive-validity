### Why efficacy failed: the target was not engaged, or it was the wrong target

A program that fails on efficacy fails in one of two ways that matter for target selection: the drug never engaged its target well enough in patients, so the target hypothesis was never tested, or it engaged the target and the disease did not respond. The cohort is every Phase 2 and 3 program that entered in 2008–2019 and stopped for efficacy or for PK, dose or formulation: as the registry records it ({{attribution.by_origin.registry_efficacy.programs|int}} efficacy, {{attribution.by_origin.registry_pk.programs|int}} PK), or as traced with a source from the stops the registry does not explain ({{attribution.by_origin.traced_efficacy.programs|int}} efficacy, {{attribution.by_origin.traced_pk.programs|int}} PK). An exposure failure is the same biology as a failure to engage, so PK stops are broken down here rather than counted apart. For each of the {{attribution.phase23.programs|int}} programs the model would label ({{attribution.unlabelled.phase23|int}} more, mostly gene and cell therapies and live biotherapeutics, were refused), an LLM read an evidence bundle: the failed trial's record, posted results and papers, the drug's other trials and their outcomes, results posted by its Phase 1 trials, its FDA approvals, other drugs on the same target in the same or a related disease, and PubMed abstracts on the drug's human pharmacology and on the drug in that disease. It judged, for humans and at the doses tested, the three pillars of [Morgan et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drudis.2011.12.020): exposure at the site of action, binding or engagement of the target, and the expected pathway effect. Engagement could also be inferred when the drug worked in another disease through the same target and tissue compartment at a comparable dose. It then coded the failure, using only the bundle and citing the sources it used. The codes and checks were fixed before any program was labelled; the full method, with examples and prompts, is in `report/methods_stops_and_engagement.md`. Where the bundle held no human evidence of engagement ({{attribution.bundle_only.groups.indeterminate.share|pct0}} of programs), a second pass, added after the first results, gave the same model the same bundle and the web: it searched full-text papers, conference reports, FDA and EMA reviews and company releases for human pharmacology, and could cite only pages its own searches returned. It decided {{attribution.search.decided|int}} of the {{attribution.search.searched|int}} programs it searched; the figures below use those labels.

Where the record shows whether the mechanism was tested, the drug had engaged its target in {{attribution.phase23.groups.engaged_no_benefit.n|int}} of {{attribution.phase23.testable.n|int}} programs and the disease did not respond; **{{attribution.phase23.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}** ({{attribution.phase23.testable.lo|pct0}}–{{attribution.phase23.testable.hi|pct0}}) failed without adequate engagement or exposure, or with another drug on the same target succeeding in the disease. The second kind is the larger and is inferred rather than measured: {{attribution.phase23.codes.F1b.n|int}} of the {{attribution.phase23.groups.not_engaged.n|int}} not-engaged programs failed where a drug on the same target worked, {{attribution.phase23.f1b_no_engagement_data|int}} of them with no human engagement data of their own; the rest had exposure or engagement measured and found inadequate. Failures to engage are about as common in Phase 3 ({{attribution.by_phase.3.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}) as in Phase 2 ({{attribution.by_phase.2.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}): no regulation requires engagement to be shown, and good practice places it in Phase 1 or 2a, yet engagement was often still unverified when confirmatory trials started. Outside oncology the share is {{attribution.outside_oncology.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}}. Trial design, population or endpoint account for {{attribution.phase23.groups.trial_design.share|pct0}} of efficacy failures, and toxicity that offset efficacy for {{attribution.phase23.groups.toxicity.n|int}} programs. Another {{attribution.reclassified.programs|int}} programs recorded as efficacy stops were not failed efficacy tests (a met endpoint, a missed non-inferiority margin, a business stop, or off-target toxicity); they are counted under their traced cause above and left out here.

* *Engaged, no benefit.* Venglustat cut glucosylceramide in the cerebrospinal fluid of people with *GBA1* Parkinson's disease by about 73% at the top dose ([Peterschmitt 2022](https://doi.org/10.3233/JPD-212714)), and over a year their motor scores worsened by 7.3 points on drug against 4.7 on placebo ([MOVES-PD](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00205-3)). CSL112, plasma-derived apolipoprotein A1 that raises cholesterol efflux capacity, did not reduce myocardial infarction, stroke or cardiovascular death at 90 days in 18,219 patients after myocardial infarction (hazard ratio 0.93, 0.81–1.05; [AEGIS-II](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2400969)).
* *Not engaged.* PF-06687234, an IL-10 fusion protein added to infliximab for ulcerative colitis, was stopped for futility, and was detected, at low concentration, in only one colon tissue sample: the drug may never have reached the inflamed mucosa at a useful level ([Ther Adv Gastroenterol 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/17562848251410784)).

**Phase 1.** Phase 1 trials mostly test safety, dose and exposure, often in healthy volunteers, so an efficacy or exposure stop there is usually an early patient cohort or a dose-finding readout. The same labels were applied to the {{attribution.phase1.programs|int}} Phase 1 programs entering 2008–2019 that stopped for efficacy or exposure. Where engagement is known ({{attribution.phase1.testable.n|int}} programs), {{attribution.phase1.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}} ({{attribution.phase1.testable.lo|pct0}}–{{attribution.phase1.testable.hi|pct0}}) were not engaged; {{attribution.phase1.groups.indeterminate.share|pct0}} have no engagement evidence either way.

**The large caveat is what the public record does not say.** For {{attribution.phase23.groups.indeterminate.share|pct0}} of efficacy failures there is still no human evidence of engagement either way, after the web search, so a drug that never engaged and a target that does not matter cannot be told apart. Engagement was shown in patients with the program's disease for only {{attribution.coverage.engaged_in_disease|pct0}} of the programs where it was shown at all, and was public or registered before the failed trial started for {{attribution.coverage.engaged_before_index|pct0}}. The gap need not be neutral: a drug that never engaged its target leaves less to publish, and engagement inferred from success in another disease can only add to the engaged side. Among the programs the web search could decide, this shows a little: {{attribution.search.not_engaged|int}} of the {{attribution.search.testable|int}} it found tested were not engaged ({{attribution.search.not_engaged_share|pct0}}), against {{attribution.bundle_only.testable.not_engaged_share|pct0}} among the programs the bundle alone decided. If every program still indeterminate were a failure to engage, the ceiling is {{attribution.bounds.all_indeterminate_not_engaged|pct0}}. **Checks.** A second model, given the same bundles and asked the pillar questions one at a time before choosing any code, relabelled a seeded random sample of {{attribution.rederivation.sampled|int}} programs ({{attribution.rederivation.n|int}} with a validated label from both): it agreed on the not-engaged / engaged / other split for {{attribution.rederivation.three_way_agree|pct0}} (κ = {{attribution.rederivation.three_way_kappa|x2}}) and on whether the target was engaged for {{attribution.rederivation.engaged_agree|pct0}}. Separately, agents with web access researched a seeded random sample of {{attribution.adjudication.sampled|int}} programs from scratch (publications, conference reports, company releases), without the pipeline's labels ({{attribution.adjudication.n|int}} also have a pipeline label): they agreed on the three-way split for {{attribution.adjudication.three_way_agree|pct0}} (κ = {{attribution.adjudication.three_way_kappa|x2}}) and on the group for {{attribution.adjudication.decided_group_agree|pct0}} of the programs the pipeline decided; they never called engaged a program the pipeline called not engaged, or the reverse ({{attribution.adjudication.contradictions|int}} contradictions). Of the {{attribution.search.adjudicated.n|int}} adjudicated programs the bundle had left indeterminate, the web search and the outside researchers both decided {{attribution.search.adjudicated.both_decided|int}} and agreed on {{attribution.search.adjudicated.three_way_agree_both_decided|pct0}} of them; the search never decided one the researchers could not, and the researchers decided {{attribution.search.adjudicated.outside_decided_search_u|int}} that the search left open ({{attribution.search.adjudicated.outside_decided_search_u_groups.not_engaged|int}} of them not engaged). On their own, they found {{attribution.adjudication.adjudicated_not_engaged|int}} failures to engage among {{attribution.adjudication.adjudicated_testable|int}} programs whose mechanism they could judge ({{attribution.adjudication.adjudicated_not_engaged_share|pct0}}). The pre-registered construct checks, on data the labels do not use, point one way or nowhere. Programs coded not engaged had germline genetic support for the disease more often than those coded engaged without benefit ({{attribution.construct.c_g3.share_F1a|pct0}} against {{attribution.construct.c_g3.share_F2|pct0}}, p = {{attribution.construct.c_g3.p|x2}}, {{attribution.construct.c_g3.n.F1a|int}} programs coded F1a), as expected if engaged failures more often test a target the disease does not depend on. The two groups did not differ in whether other drugs on their target failed ({{attribution.construct.a_same_target_failed.share_F1a|pct0}} against {{attribution.construct.a_same_target_failed.share_F2|pct0}}) or advanced ({{attribution.construct.b_same_target_advanced.share_F1a|pct0}} against {{attribution.construct.b_same_target_advanced.share_F2|pct0}}) in the disease.

The answer to "was the target wrong, or was it never hit?" is therefore: where anyone can tell, the target was usually hit. Among efficacy failures whose mechanism was tested, {{attribution.bounds.labelled|pct0}} failed to engage ({{attribution.sensitivity_range.lo|pct0}} to {{attribution.sensitivity_range.hi|pct0}} across the variants above); in the rest the drug engaged its target and the disease did not move. For {{attribution.phase23.groups.indeterminate.share|pct0}} of failures the public record, searched, cannot say.
