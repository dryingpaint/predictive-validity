### Why efficacy failed: the target was not engaged, or it was the wrong target

A program that fails on efficacy fails in one of two ways that matter for target selection: the drug never engaged its target well enough in patients, so the target hypothesis was never tested, or it engaged the target and the disease did not respond. For each of the {{attribution.phase23.programs|int}} Phase 2 and Phase 3 programs above that stopped for efficacy and that the model would label ({{attribution.unlabelled.phase23|int}} more, mostly gene and cell therapies and live biotherapeutics, were refused), an LLM read an evidence bundle: the failed trial's record, posted results and papers, the drug's other trials and their outcomes, results posted by its Phase 1 trials, its FDA approvals, other drugs on the same target in the same or a related disease, and PubMed abstracts on the drug's human pharmacology and on the drug in that disease. It judged, for humans and at the doses tested, the three pillars of [Morgan et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drudis.2011.12.020): exposure at the site of action, binding or engagement of the target, and the expected pathway effect. Engagement could also be inferred when the drug worked in another disease through the same target and tissue compartment at a comparable dose. It then coded the failure, using only the bundle and citing the sources it used. The plan was fixed before any program was labelled (`report/attribution_plan.md`).

* *Engaged, no benefit.* Venglustat cut glucosylceramide in the cerebrospinal fluid of people with *GBA1* Parkinson's disease by about 73% at the top dose ([Peterschmitt 2022](https://doi.org/10.3233/JPD-212714)), and over a year their motor scores worsened by 7.3 points on drug against 4.7 on placebo ([MOVES-PD](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00205-3)). CSL112, plasma-derived apolipoprotein A1 that raises cholesterol efflux capacity, did not reduce myocardial infarction, stroke or cardiovascular death at 90 days in 18,219 patients after myocardial infarction (hazard ratio 0.93, 0.81–1.05; [AEGIS-II](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2400969)).
* *Not engaged.* PF-06687234, an IL-10 fusion protein added to infliximab for ulcerative colitis, was stopped for futility, and was detected, at low concentration, in only one colon tissue sample: the drug may never have reached the inflamed mucosa at a useful level ([Ther Adv Gastroenterol 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/17562848251410784)).

**The large caveat is what the public record does not say.** For {{attribution.phase23.groups.indeterminate.share|pct0}} of efficacy failures there is no human evidence of engagement either way, so a drug that never engaged and a target that does not matter cannot be told apart. Engagement was shown in patients with the program's disease for only {{attribution.coverage.engaged_in_disease|pct0}} of the programs where it was shown at all, and was public or registered before the failed trial started for {{attribution.coverage.engaged_before_index|pct0}}. The gap need not be neutral: a drug that never engaged its target leaves less to publish, and engagement inferred from success in another disease can only add to the engaged side. When outside researchers were given {{attribution.adjudication.pipeline_u|int}} programs the pipeline left indeterminate (below), they could decide {{attribution.adjudication.pipeline_u_decided_outside|int}}, and non-engagement was more common among those than among the programs the pipeline decided. If the indeterminate programs split the way those did, non-engagement is about {{attribution.bounds.indeterminate_split_as_adjudicated|pct0}} of failures whose mechanism was tested; if every indeterminate program were a failure to engage, the ceiling is {{attribution.bounds.all_indeterminate_not_engaged|pct0}}. The answer to "was the target wrong, or was it never hit?" is therefore: where anyone can tell, the target was usually hit. Among efficacy failures whose mechanism was tested, {{attribution.bounds.labelled|pct0}} failed to engage ({{attribution.sensitivity_range.lo|pct0}} to {{attribution.sensitivity_range.hi|pct0}} across the variants above, about {{attribution.bounds.indeterminate_split_as_adjudicated|pct0}} if the indeterminate programs resemble those outside research could decide); in the rest the drug engaged its target and the disease did not move.
