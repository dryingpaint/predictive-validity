### Design

The cohort is the {{section3.c2.programs|int}} programs on a human protein target that entered Phase 2 in 2008–2019. A program *advanced* if a Phase 3 trial of its own started, or the FDA approved it, within four years ({{section3.c2.s4|pct0}} did). Phase 2 is the first test of efficacy in patients, so it is where the evidence's claim about the disease is first put to the test. The analysis plan was registered before any outcome was examined; it named 2015–2020 entries, and the cohort was widened to the report's window so that every section describes the same programs. Other departures are marked; methods and robustness checks are in the appendix.

### Which evidence translates

The plan registered fifteen rules a sponsor could apply at Phase 2 entry, but several are narrower versions of others, so they are grouped by kind of evidence: each kind is tested against programs without it (Holm-corrected across the {{section3.types_family|int}} kinds), and each narrower version against the rest of its kind. An approved drug on the target for a related disease is shown as a benchmark. The figure sets each kind beside its relative success in reaching approval (Section 2).
