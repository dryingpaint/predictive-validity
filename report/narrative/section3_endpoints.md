Across {{section3.trials.n|int}} randomised industry efficacy trials, {{section3.trials.not_met|pct0}} missed their governing primary endpoint. Regulatory surrogates were missed least ({{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_all.not_met|pct0}}); outside oncology, exploratory biomarkers ({{section3.endpoints.B2_exploratory_biomarker_all.not_met|pct0}}) and hard outcomes ({{section3.endpoints.B8_hard_outcome_all.not_met|pct0}}) were missed most, and oncology's response and survival endpoints as often.

Genetic support for the disease lowered the chance of a miss most on regulatory surrogates (risk ratio {{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_all.rr|x2}}; {{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_fdaaa.rr|x2}} in trials with registry-posted results since FDAAA) and on clinician-rated scales ({{section3.endpoints.B5_clinician_scale_all.rr|x2}}). Objective physiology ({{section3.endpoints.B4_objective_physiological_all.rr|x2}}) and disease events ({{section3.endpoints.B7_disease_events_all.rr|x2}}) point the same way with intervals spanning 1; exploratory biomarkers ({{section3.endpoints.B2_exploratory_biomarker_all.rr|x2}}), patient-reported outcomes ({{section3.endpoints.B6_patient_reported_all.rr|x2}}, only {{section3.endpoints.B6_patient_reported_all.n|int}} genetically supported trials) and oncology's response and progression endpoints show no benefit. Too few genetically supported trials had hard-outcome or overall-survival primaries to estimate. One reading is that genetic support predicts most reliably that a drug will move the measures nearest its biology, often the trait through which the genetics was found (LDL cholesterol, urate, glycaemia), and less reliably that it will change what patients feel or how often events happen.

The endpoint classifier read each trial's registered primary outcomes without the drug. In an audit of {{section3.endpoint_audit.trials|int}} trials stratified by class, it picked the governing outcome correctly in {{section3.endpoint_audit.governing_index_correct|int}} and the class in {{section3.endpoint_audit.class_correct|int}}. Not knowing the drug, it cannot apply the codebook's drug-specific rules: FEV1 is a regulatory surrogate only for bronchodilators, and target engagement means the drug's own target.
