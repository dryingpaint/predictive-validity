Across {{section3.trials.n|int}} randomised industry efficacy trials, {{section3.trials.not_met|pct0}} missed their governing primary endpoint. Regulatory surrogates were missed least ({{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_all.not_met|pct0}}); outside oncology, exploratory biomarkers ({{section3.endpoints.B2_exploratory_biomarker_all.not_met|pct0}}) and hard outcomes ({{section3.endpoints.B8_hard_outcome_all.not_met|pct0}}) were missed most, and oncology's response and survival endpoints as often.

In trials with registry-posted results since FDAAA, genetic support for the disease lowered the chance of missing a regulatory surrogate (risk ratio {{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_fdaaa.rr|x2}}), but across all readable trials, including those whose result was read from a paper, it did not ({{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_all.rr|x2}}). Clinician-rated scales ({{section3.endpoints.B5_clinician_scale_all.rr|x2}}, {{section3.endpoints.B5_clinician_scale_all.lo|x2}}–{{section3.endpoints.B5_clinician_scale_all.hi|x2}}), objective physiology ({{section3.endpoints.B4_objective_physiological_all.rr|x2}}) and disease events ({{section3.endpoints.B7_disease_events_all.rr|x2}}) point towards fewer misses with intervals reaching 1; exploratory biomarkers ({{section3.endpoints.B2_exploratory_biomarker_all.rr|x2}}) and oncology's response and progression endpoints show no benefit. The endpoint a trial is judged on does not, on this evidence, decide whether genetic support helps.
