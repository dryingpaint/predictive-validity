Across {{section3.trials.n|int}} randomised industry efficacy trials, {{section3.trials.not_met|pct0}} missed their governing primary endpoint. Regulatory surrogates were missed least ({{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_all.not_met|pct0}}), exploratory biomarkers ({{section3.endpoints.B2_exploratory_biomarker_all.not_met|pct0}}) and hard outcomes ({{section3.endpoints.B8_hard_outcome_all.not_met|pct0}}) most.

Genetic support for the disease lowered the chance of a miss most on regulatory surrogates (risk ratio {{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_all.rr|x2}}; {{section3.endpoints.B3_regulatory_surrogate_fdaaa.rr|x2}} in trials with registry-posted results since FDAAA) and on clinician-rated scales ({{section3.endpoints.B5_clinician_scale_all.rr|x2}}). Objective physiology ({{section3.endpoints.B4_objective_physiological_all.rr|x2}}) and disease events ({{section3.endpoints.B7_disease_events_all.rr|x2}}) point the same way with intervals spanning 1; exploratory biomarkers ({{section3.endpoints.B2_exploratory_biomarker_all.rr|x2}}), patient-reported outcomes ({{section3.endpoints.B6_patient_reported_all.rr|x2}}, only {{section3.endpoints.B6_patient_reported_all.n|int}} genetically supported trials) and oncology's response and progression endpoints show no benefit. Too few genetically supported trials had hard-outcome or overall-survival primaries to estimate. One reading is that genetic support predicts most reliably that a drug will move the measures nearest its biology, often the trait through which the genetics was found (LDL cholesterol, urate, glycaemia), and less reliably that it will change what patients feel or how often events happen.

At the program level, classifying each Phase 2 program by the endpoint of its first randomised Phase 2 efficacy trial (started within a year of entry and before it advanced), genetic support gives {{section3.twin.proximal.rr|x2}} ({{section3.twin.proximal.lo|x2}}–{{section3.twin.proximal.hi|x2}}; {{section3.twin.proximal.programs|int}} programs) where that endpoint was proximal and {{section3.twin.intermediate.rr|x2}} ({{section3.twin.intermediate.lo|x2}}–{{section3.twin.intermediate.hi|x2}}; {{section3.twin.intermediate.programs|int}}) where it was intermediate; distal-endpoint and oncology programs were too few.

The endpoint classifier read each trial's registered primary outcomes without the drug. In an audit of 40 trials stratified by class, it picked the governing outcome correctly in all 40 and the class in 39. Not knowing the drug, it puts FEV1 in the regulatory-surrogate class for every respiratory drug, where the codebook reserves that for bronchodilators; moving the 74 affected trials to the intermediate class barely moves the H4 estimate. Trials still in follow-up with results posted have no outcome label yet and are not counted (about 2% of the H4 trials).
