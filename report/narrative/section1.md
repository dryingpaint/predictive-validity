### How programs are counted

{{section1.scale.cohort_programs|int}} programs of {{section1.scale.cohort_entities|int}} individual drugs entered at least one phase in 2008–2019. Entries end in 2019 so that every program has had at least six years to advance or stop before the snapshot; registration of industry trials became mandatory in the US in late 2007, so the window starts in 2008. A program is one molecular entity developed for one indication; it enters a phase when its first industry trial of that phase starts, and *advances* when a later-phase trial in the disease starts or the FDA approves it for the disease. As in BIO's report, each phase's success rate is advanced ÷ (advanced + stopped), and LOA is their product. The full counting rules are in the appendix.
