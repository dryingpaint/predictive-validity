Disease areas rank differently from BIO's: the rank correlation with BIO's LOA across the {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.area_loa_n|int}} areas is {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.area_loa|x2}} for all programs and {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.area_us_loa|x2}} for US-developed ones. Hematology and metabolic programs reach approval most often in both reports ({{section1.area.hematology.loa|pct}} and {{section1.area.metabolic.loa|pct}} across all programs), and urology programs least. Among US-developed programs, oncology ({{section1.area.oncology.us_loa|pct}}) and endocrine programs ({{section1.area.endocrine.us_loa|pct}}) come out far above BIO's {{bio.oncology}} and {{bio.endocrine}}; for oncology, the 2015–2025 window holds the wave of immunotherapy, antibody–drug conjugate and bispecific approvals, and an approval in a cancer subtype counts for the program's cancer. Infectious disease ({{section1.area.infectious_disease.us_loa|pct}}) sits far below BIO's {{bio.infectious_disease}}, largely because many registered anti-infective and COVID-19 programs, including US-run ones, were never approved; ophthalmology ({{section1.area.ophthalmology.us_loa|pct}}) and gastroenterology ({{section1.area.gastroenterology.us_loa|pct}}) also fall below BIO's {{bio.ophthalmology}} and {{bio.gastroenterology}}. Among modalities, RNA interference, multispecific antibodies and antibody–drug conjugates reach approval most often across all programs ({{section1.modality.sirna_rnai.loa|pct}}, {{section1.modality.multispecific_antibody.loa|pct}} and {{section1.modality.antibody_drug_conjugate.loa|pct}}), and among the classes BIO also reports, non-antibody proteins ({{section1.modality.protein.loa|pct}}) and small molecules ({{section1.modality.small_molecule.loa|pct}}) least. The modality ordering agrees with BIO's only loosely (rank correlation {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.modality_loa|x2}}), mostly because CAR-T ranks lower here ({{section1.modality.car_t.loa|pct}} against BIO's {{bio.car_t}}) and antisense oligonucleotides higher. Strata with few programs have wide intervals.
