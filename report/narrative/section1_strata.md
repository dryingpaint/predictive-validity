Disease areas rank differently from BIO's: the rank correlation with BIO's LOA across the {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.area_loa_n|int}} areas is {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.area_loa|x2}} for all programs and {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.area_us_loa|x2}} for US-developed ones. Across all programs, hematology, metabolic and oncology programs reach approval most often ({{section1.area.hematology.loa|pct}}, {{section1.area.metabolic.loa|pct}} and {{section1.area.oncology.loa|pct}}), and cardiovascular, gastroenterology and urology programs least ({{section1.area.cardiovascular.loa|pct}}, {{section1.area.gastroenterology.loa|pct}} and {{section1.area.urology.loa|pct}}). Among US-developed programs, endocrine ({{section1.area.endocrine.us_loa|pct}}) and oncology programs ({{section1.area.oncology.us_loa|pct}}) come out far above BIO's {{bio.endocrine}} and {{bio.oncology}}; for oncology, the window takes in the wave of immunotherapy and antibody–drug conjugate approvals, and an approval in a cancer subtype counts for the program's cancer. Ophthalmology ({{section1.area.ophthalmology.us_loa|pct}}) falls well below BIO's {{bio.ophthalmology}}. Among modalities, monoclonal antibodies and antibody–drug conjugates reach approval most often across all programs ({{section1.modality.monoclonal_antibody.loa|pct}} and {{section1.modality.antibody_drug_conjugate.loa|pct}}), and among the classes BIO also reports, antisense oligonucleotides ({{section1.modality.antisense_oligonucleotide.loa|pct}}), peptides ({{section1.modality.peptide.loa|pct}}) and gene therapies ({{section1.modality.gene_therapy.loa|pct}}) least. RNA interference and CAR-T, near the top of BIO's ordering, have too few entities resolved in Phase 3 here (most of their approvals came straight from Phase 2) to estimate. Across the {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.modality_loa_n|int}} classes both estimate, the modality ordering agrees with BIO's (rank correlation {{section1.rank_correlation_with_bio.modality_loa|x2}}).
