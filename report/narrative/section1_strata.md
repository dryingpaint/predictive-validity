The ordering of disease areas broadly follows BIO's: hematology ({{section1.area.hematology.us_loa|pct}} for US-developed programs) and metabolic programs ({{section1.area.metabolic.us_loa|pct}}) reach approval most often, and cardiovascular, gastroenterology and urology programs least. Oncology ({{section1.area.oncology.us_loa|pct}}) comes out well above BIO's {{bio.oncology}}: the 2015–2025 window holds the wave of immunotherapy, antibody–drug conjugate and bispecific approvals, and an approval in a cancer subtype counts for the program's cancer. Infectious disease ({{section1.area.infectious_disease.us_loa|pct}}) sits far below BIO's {{bio.infectious_disease}}, largely because many registered anti-infective and COVID-19 programs, including US-run ones, were never approved. Among modalities, RNA interference, multispecific antibodies and antibody–drug conjugates reach approval most often and small molecules and non-antibody proteins least, the same broad pattern BIO reported. Strata with few programs have wide intervals.
