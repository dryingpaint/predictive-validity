**Figure (main). Did the preclinical models predict what happened in patients?**
**a**, For each of the four questions a preclinical model is asked, the headline status of the cell across every tile of the atlas. A tile is one mechanism (target, action) in one disease, tracked when at least three programs entered Phase 2 for it in 2008–2019; the number after the mechanism is its programs. Every model system compared with patients for the class is a track; a cell's headline is the best latest status among its tracks that predicted before the patient result, never compared when none did, and a retrodiction never sets it. Matched: a published comparison showed the model's prediction held in patients; hatched, the model's own data had put a drug of the class below its usual effect bar and that drug failed, a known failure the model called. Contradicted: it failed. A dose failure hatched "through efficacy" is a drug that reached its exposure target and still failed, the efficacy failure seen from the dose cell, which the rules count once. **b**, The fifteen diseases with the largest global burden in 2021 (disability-adjusted life years, WHO Global Health Estimates; `lists/burden.csv` gives the mapping of each disease to a WHO cause and its grade), one tile each: the mechanism in that disease with the most programs. The mark is the headline status, the bold year is when it was earned, then the model that set it. A contradicted cell carries, on a second line, why it failed. An open red triangle marks a matched cell in which another model's prediction failed, with that model's finding in grey; a small open square marks an efficacy cell whose surrogate matched while the outcome trial was negative. Statuses were drafted and checked by separate agents and adjudicated; when a cell is split by drug modality the worst strip is shown. Every other tile is in the supplementary figure; timelines, quotes and sources are in the interactive atlas. Source: `preclin.atlas_track_entry` and `atlas_track_verdict`; drafts in `analyses/model-system-atlas/drafts/`.
