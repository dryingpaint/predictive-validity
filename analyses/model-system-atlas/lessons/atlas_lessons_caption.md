# Figure: what a preclinical "yes" was worth

**What a preclinical "yes" was worth.** Every tile of the atlas reached Phase 2, so a model that said no and was believed is absent; nothing here is a sensitivity. Statuses are the atlas's adjudicated statuses. **a**, Tiles whose engagement headline matched (83 of 166), by their efficacy headline, split by class outcome (approved, never approved), by the tier of the headline engagement readout (direct: occupancy or free target; proximal: the target's own product; downstream: a biomarker further down) and by disease area with three or more such tiles. At the right, the contradicted share with its Wilson 95 % interval. Engagement held in 40 of 78 approved and 43 of 88 never-approved classes (Fisher p = 0.88); the tier contrast is not significant (chi-square p = 0.33). **b**, The 127 safety contradictions with an admissible comparison, typed from the entry text by two coders (the second blind; agreement on type 107 of 114, kappa 0.90; on harm class 107 of 114, kappa 0.93) as silent on the harm (solid), stated there would be none (hatched) or a harm patients never had (violet), by harm class. Each (model system, harm, type) is counted once, so a toxicology package behind three tiles is one silence: 107 systems, 137 system–harm pairs. "Metabolic, endocrine, bone" includes bone density. Source: `analyses/model-system-atlas/lessons/` (`lessons.py all`); checks in `lessons/robustness.md`.

# Supplementary figure: the family rates

**Family rates.** For each family of model system (keyword rules over the atlas's model descriptions with a hand-reviewed override file; `lessons/families.py`), **a**, the share of compared tracks whose positive efficacy prediction the trial confirmed and, **b**, the share of safety predictions that held, each with a 95 % percentile interval from 2,000 cluster-bootstrap resamples of tiles; the dashed line is the pooled rate; families with fewer than four tiles are listed below the rule without an interval; at the right, matched / compared tracks and the number of tiles. A family's rate follows the classes it served (`family_rates_by_outcome.csv` splits every family by class outcome), so the panels say which families' yes to discount, not which family predicts.
