# Figure: what a preclinical "yes" was worth, by family of model system

**What a preclinical "yes" was worth, by family of model system.** Every tile of the atlas reached Phase 2, so a model that said no and was believed is absent; the rates are what a positive prediction was worth once the drug was in patients, never a sensitivity. **a**, Efficacy. For each family of model system (keyword rules over the atlas's model descriptions with a hand-reviewed override file; `lessons/families.py`), the share of compared tracks whose positive efficacy prediction the trial confirmed (matched / (matched + contradicted)), with a 95 % percentile interval from 2,000 cluster-bootstrap resamples of tiles, since the tracks of one tile share one trial outcome. The dashed line is the pooled rate over every family. Families with fewer than four tiles are listed below the rule without an interval. At the right, matched / compared tracks and the number of tiles. **b**, Safety, same layout: the share of each family's safety predictions that held, where a prediction is either a stated finding or silence on a harm the species could have shown (brief v3). Three contradictions the review typed as having no admissible comparison are left out. **c**, Tiles whose engagement headline matched (105 of 119), by their efficacy headline, split by the engagement readout's tier (direct: occupancy or free target; proximal: the target's own product; downstream: a biomarker further down) and by disease area. **d**, The 73 typed safety contradictions by harm class, each harm counted once per track: the model was silent on the harm (solid), stated there would be none (hatched), or predicted a harm patients never had (violet). "Metabolic / endocrine" includes bone. Statuses are the atlas's adjudicated statuses; nothing is re-adjudicated here. Source: `analyses/model-system-atlas/lessons/` (`lessons.py all`).
