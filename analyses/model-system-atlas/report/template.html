How this was made

The diseases are the most-developed of each therapeutic area by Phase 2 entrants of 2008–2019. Each novel Phase 2 program counts toward one mechanism: its verified target and action, pooled across modalities, from a hand review of every drug that decides a tracked mechanism. A mechanism with at least three programs in an indication is tracked; its track record is drafted by one agent, re-checked source by source by a second, and disagreements are settled by a third under the written rules (rules v3).

A cell is matched when a published comparison shows the model's prediction held in patients, contradicted when it failed, and never compared when the model was used but nobody compared it. A retrodiction reproduced a result patients had already shown. A failed model stays failed in its own record; a cell turns back to matched only when a different model took over, or when the early patient signal itself faded.
