## Scope notes

The healthy-volunteer drug–drug interaction trial has no treatment, prevention, or diagnostic indication and therefore produces no asset–indication queries. Its verified therapeutic-primary targets remain eligible for target-only discovery.

The target-only counts use only stored, unambiguous aliases. Protein-family and complex component membership is reported as provenance but is not expanded into asserted target aliases. Broad nucleic-acid targets are excluded by policy.
